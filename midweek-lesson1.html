🔤 Pinyin Focus · This Week

1. The 4 Tones + Neutral Tone

mā
妈 · 1st
má
麻 · 2nd
mǎ
马 · 3rd
mà
骂 · 4th
ma
吗 · neutral

1st = high & flat · 2nd = rising · 3rd = dip (down then up) · 4th = falling · neutral = light & short

⚡ 3rd-tone change: When two 3rd tones meet, the first one becomes a 2nd tone.
nǐ + hǎo → ní hǎo (你好 sounds like ní hǎo, not nǐ hǎo!)
