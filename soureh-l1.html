苏蕾的第一课

Lesson 1 · 听标准发音，自己跟读，录下来对比
苏蕾，你好！ 这一页的每一句话都来自你自己的介绍——你的名字、你的家乡、你的家人、你的女儿。先听标准发音，再自己跟读，录下来对比。

1 · Tone Reference 四声参考

Reference only: feel the difference between the four tones. Your real practice is in Section 2 — sentences from your own story.

2 · Your Sentences 你的句子

Every sentence comes from your own introduction. Listen, then repeat out loud and record yourself. 本课重点：j · x · sh · ü

3 · How to Practice 怎么练

1
Listen

Tap Listen and hear the standard pronunciation.

2
Record yourself

Tap Record, speak after the audio, then tap Stop.

3
Compare & repeat

Tap Compare to hear the standard version, then your voice. Repeat until they match. Save your best recording and send it to your teacher.

On iPhone: if sound or recording does not work inside a chat app, open this page in Safari instead. Allow microphone access when asked.
