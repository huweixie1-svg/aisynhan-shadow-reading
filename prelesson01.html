What is AISYNHAN?

AISYNHAN is an AI-powered Chinese learning platform for learners around the world.

The name comes from two parts:

  • Syn = "together, connected" — like the ancient Silk Road (丝绸之路) that linked cultures
  • Han = 汉, the Chinese language and culture

Think of it as a digital Silk Road for learning Chinese.

How can AISYNHAN help you learn Chinese?

  • AI practice partner — practice speaking, vocabulary, and sentences anytime, no need to wait for a teacher
  • Learn at your level — lessons adapt to you, step by step
  • Track your progress — record your goals and reflections before and after each lesson
  • Real-life situations — learn practical Chinese for daily conversations
