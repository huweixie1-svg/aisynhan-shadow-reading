Shadow Reading Practice

跟读练习 · 听标准发音，自己跟读，录下来对比
Sample lesson. These words are examples of what a lesson looks like after your self-introduction is added. Once your teacher has your story, every word here will come from your own life — your name, your city, your family, your hobbies.

1 · The Four Tones 四声

In Chinese, the tone of a syllable changes its meaning. Listen to each tone, record yourself, and compare until yours sounds natural.

2 · Today's Words 本课词语

Words from your story. Listen, then repeat out loud and record yourself.

3 · How to Practice 怎么练

1
Listen

Tap Listen and hear the standard pronunciation.

2
Record yourself

Tap Record, speak after the audio, then tap Stop.

3
Compare & repeat

Tap Compare to hear the standard version, then your voice. Repeat until they match. Save your best recording and send it to your teacher.

On iPhone: if sound or recording does not work inside a chat app, open this page in Safari instead. Allow microphone access when asked.
